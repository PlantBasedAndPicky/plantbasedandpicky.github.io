Costa Coffee

📍 Manchester Arndale Centre
Veggie / Vegan Options
Overall Experience ⭐⭐⭐⭐

At a Glance

  • ✨ Standout Pick: Vegan Ham & Cheese Toastie
  • ☕ Signature Drink: Soya Iced Chai Latte
  • 🛋️ Vibe: Busy High-Street Coffee Spot
  • 💷 Price: £

Our Thoughts

We were thrilled to see plant-based meat on the menu!

My husband and I both prefer soya milk over cow's milk, and we love that you can order almost any drink here with a choice of soya, oat, or coconut milk. My absolute go-to is a Soya Iced Chai Latte.

Vegans are not overwhelmed with choice, but you will certainly find something available here, both toasties and pastries. Veggies fare slightly better with some cheese-based options.

Today's Order

  • Soya Iced Chai Latte & Soya Hot Chocolate (Drinks): It’s fantastic having these options readily available. The hot chocolate usually comes with regular whipped cream and marshmallows, which we decline for obvious reasons. However, a few places we've visited have offered vegan whipped cream, so it’s always worth asking just in case!
  • Vegan Ham & Cheese Toastie & Egg & Mushroom Bap (Breakfast): It's probably obvious who ordered what! The vegan cheese on the toastie was melted to absolute perfection; honestly, it melts so beautifully that I always feel the need to double-check that it's actually vegan.

You certainly will not go hungry at Costa Coffee with the array of pastries and cakes available.
