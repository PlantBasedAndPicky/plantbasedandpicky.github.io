Read Our Story ▾

People always say that if you can’t find what you’re looking for, become the thing you want to see and that’s exactly what this is.

My husband and I have been vegetarian for over six years, and we love exploring new places to eat. We binge food‑review channels on YouTube and Instagram. But there’s always been one thing missing.

No one ever talks about the vegetarian or vegan option. They don’t mention whether the restaurant accommodates different dietary requirements. They don’t show what’s available for people who choose to eat differently, whether for health, ethics, sustainability, or simply preference. It’s like the veggie option is an afterthought, or worse, invisible.

And honestly? We’re tired of waiting.

So this is us stepping up. This is us becoming the thing we’ve been looking for.

This is a space to highlight the veggie option, to show people what’s actually out there, to celebrate restaurants that make an effort, and to help anyone who’s actively choosing what they eat feel seen, supported, and included. Whether you’re vegetarian, vegan, flexitarian, or just curious, you deserve to know what’s available and where to find it.
