Pizza Hut

📍 Manchester
Veggie Options
Overall Experience ⭐⭐
⚠️ Rating Clarification: Our low rating reflects the severe lack of vegetarian and vegan choices, not the quality of the food itself. This score is based strictly on how accommodating and diverse the menu is for plant-based diners.

At a Glance

  • ✨ Standout Feature: The Salad Bar
  • 🛋️ Vibe: Standard Chain Restaurant
  • 💷 Price: ££

Our Thoughts

Vegans, give this place a miss.

The choices for vegetarians are incredibly limited, and for vegans, they are practically nonexistent. The menu offers only a few basic pizzas, like a Veggie Supreme or a Vegan Margherita, and the "create your own" feature merely recycles those same limited ingredients. While the salad bar has a decent variety, the overall food selection falls completely short.

Most disappointingly, there are absolutely no plant-based meat alternatives on offer—a standard option that is readily available at almost every other pizza restaurant today. If you do find yourself here, you won't go entirely hungry. We spotted one vegan light bite (which heavily resembles the standard pizza options) and a single vegan dessert on the menu.

Just a quick note, the staff were great and did help us with any questions we had.

What we ordered:

  • Buffet All-In (Includes salad bar, bottomless drink, and ice cream): We chose this specifically to sample a variety of veggie and vegan options. The biggest disappointment? There was not a single vegan pizza put out on the buffet line. Two of the veggie pizzas there were the exact same, just with a different style base. There was pasta available, but it was lukewarm and definitely not worth getting.

Note: There are far better places to eat in Manchester for plant-based diners. Save your time and head elsewhere.
